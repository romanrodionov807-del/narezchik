Narezchik

A personal desktop tool for creating short vertical videos.

Narezchik runs locally on its owner's computer. It turns the owner's long videos into short vertical clips with subtitles and, at the owner's explicit request, publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

It is not a public service: it has no user accounts, no server, and it only posts to the TikTok account that its owner connects through TikTok Login Kit.
