Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Summary

Narezchik runs only on its owner's computer. It has no servers and does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.

2. Data from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, Narezchik receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) to show which account is connected and to publish videos you choose to publish. The token is stored only in a local file on your computer and is used only to call the TikTok API on your behalf. Disconnecting the account in Narezchik deletes the local token; you can also revoke access in your TikTok settings.

3. Videos

Videos and their transcripts are processed and stored locally on your computer. A video is sent to TikTok only when you choose to publish it.

4. Third parties

Narezchik sends data only to TikTok (to publish the videos you select) and, if you enable it, to the AI service you configure for choosing highlights. No data is used for advertising or analytics.
